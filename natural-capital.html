Evidence on your natural assets, without sending a surveyor to every site.

Plantiverse reads land, forests, estates and the green areas of infrastructure from space and from the ground, and returns indicators of state and change where every figure carries its source, its date and its confidence.

Who it is for

Owners and managers of land and forests, estates, logistics and industrial sites, energy and transport infrastructure with green areas, and the funds, lenders and advisers who assess them. The common need: to show what is happening to natural assets, regularly, without repeating costly site visits.

What you get in 4 to 6 weeks

  • Time series of vegetation indices from the public Copernicus Sentinel-2 archive (10 m resolution on the visible and near-infrared bands), aligned with weather and the history of the site.
  • Change maps and anomalies, each one explained or flagged for a visit.
  • A report and platform access where every figure shows source, date and confidence, plus the check that would prove it wrong.
  • A plain statement of limits: what a satellite sees well, what it does not, and where a ground check is needed.

How it works

  1. A 30-minute call to fix the question and the sites (one to three for the pilot).
  2. You send the site boundaries (KML or shapefile) and what you already hold: surveys, inventories, reports.
  3. We build the series and the first read; you review it with us in the third week.
  4. Final report and access to the platform.

No hardware is needed. If you want ground checks, we can ship our MAPIR multispectral cameras to you.

What it is not

It is not a certification, not a carbon-credit issuance and not a replacement for your qualified auditor, forester or agronomist. It is evidence to work with them, built to be checked.

Where the numbers come from

Every claim on this site has a source. Our models are reported together with how much they add over the simplest prediction, and the record page lists what we claim and what we do not. The platform learns from real sites, among them the green roof of ENEA in Rome.

Next step

Write to us with the type of site, its size and what you need to show. After a short call we propose a scope of work; the effort depends on the sites.
